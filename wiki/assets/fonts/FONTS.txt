Fonts bundled with Westernis
============================

These fonts are third-party works under their own licences. They are NOT
covered by the project's AGPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY-SA 4.0 licences.
Each licence file starts with the font's own copyright notice and is copied
verbatim from the upstream repository (google/fonts for the OFL families).

All WOFF2 files are lossless wawoff2 conversions of the upstream TTFs; the
font data is unchanged (no subsetting, no renaming, so the Reserved Font
Names may stay; see OFL-FAQ 2.2). After decompression every table is
byte-identical to the upstream TTF, except glyf, loca and head, which the
WOFF2 glyph transform re-encodes, and the empty DSIG signature table, which
WOFF2 encoders drop. The variable fonts keep all their weights.

Other uses of these fonts in the repository
-------------------------------------------
- tools/brand/fonts/Cinzel[wght].ttf is the upstream Cinzel TTF (OFL-1.1, licence
  next to it), the input of tools/brand/make-wordmark.js.
- content/files/Westernis_map.svg embeds glyph subsets of Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative
  Bold and IM FELL English Roman/Italic under the family names WstMapCaps,
  WstMapTitle, WstMapRoman and WstMapHand. Embedding in a document is allowed by
  OFL-FAQ 1.12-1.13; the subsets keep their copyright and licence metadata.
- The logo, wordmark and tagline SVGs in wiki/assets/brand contain Cinzel outlines
  converted to paths (artwork, OFL-FAQ 1.1 and 1.13).
